## Description (4000)
Skullset is a simple tracker for Greyskull LP, phrakture's variant. There are no programs to choose and no clutter. Open it and lift.

THE PROGRAM
• Workout A: Overhead Press 3×5+, Chin-ups 3×5+, Squat 3×5+
• Workout B: Bench Press 3×5+, Barbell Row 3×5+, Deadlift 1×5+
• Workouts alternate A and B. The last set of each lift is as many reps as possible.

AUTOMATIC PROGRESSION
• Get all your reps: the weight goes up next time.
• 10 or more reps on the last set: the weight goes up twice as much.
• Miss reps: the weight goes down 10%.
• Chin-ups track your reps.

BUILT FOR THE GYM
• Tap a set to log 5 reps. Tap again to take off a rep. On the last set, pick how many reps you did.
• The rest timer starts when you log a set, and a notification tells you when to lift again.
• The plate calculator shows the plates for each side of the bar.
• Choose the plates your gym has, and weights are rounded so you can load them.
• Use kg or lb.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• History of every workout.
• Charts for each lift.
• Apple Health: Skullset can read your bodyweight and save your workouts.

PRIVATE
Skullset has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your device.
