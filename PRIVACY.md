# Skullset privacy policy

Skullset does not collect, send or share any of your data.

- Your workouts and settings stay on your device.
- If you give permission, Skullset reads your bodyweight from Apple Health and saves your finished workouts to Apple Health. This data does not leave your device through Skullset.
- Skullset has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

You can remove Apple Health access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.
